2. **Owned additive storage:** three nullable/no-default columns `entry_confluences`, `entry_confluences_source`, `entry_confluences_recorded_at`; no legacy backfill. Separate `confluence_labels` user-keyed table persists reusable names even after deselection/deleting a trade, with user FK cascade. Existing tags, mistakes, emotion, model, checklist and screenshot references stay separate and are never copied into labels. Bots without a human declaration remain unknown. Declarations can be retrospective, including labels on a newly recorded historical trade. Imports receive new IDs/time/source; backup owners, source and claimed old clocks are not replayed. Confluence edits do not validate the original entry, make label history immutable, or automatically invalidate/review previously declared reasons after a financial correction.
