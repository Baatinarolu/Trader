## Saved course basis and limits

Saved **Ep29** (S3), the Flow AI episode, recorded at low priority and mainly as the fifth episode requiring M79. The case for an assistant in the episode is **speed of feedback** — *"the real edge of AI is speed. It helps you close the gap faster between confusion and clarity, emotion and logic, mistake and lesson, impulse and discipline"* — and the moment it names is **mid-session, tilted, about to click**: *"when you are trading… the minute price starts going to draw down, this is where I start feeling some form of emotion… come to Flow AI. Flow AI will ground you."* The audit recorded the gap precisely: the codebase already produces the substance (the `coach.js` insights, the guardrail state the entry engine reads, the trader's own records) but there is **no route by which a user can ask the system anything**, and no LLM integration point of any kind.

The episode also draws the line this milestone refuses to cross, and the audit quoted it: *"AI should not replace your trade plan. It should not replace your journaling. It should not replace your review process. It should not replace your risk management. It should not replace your personal responsibility… if you start using AI as a shortcut for certainty, you are using it wrong"* and *"AI is not the most powerful when it tries to predict the market for you. Trust me, I've tried it."* Flow AI v1 *"is not some magic AI that can read your trading account"* — and the audit's own fairness note says the codebase is **ahead** of Flow AI v1 on data-driven personal feedback, because it already reads the trade database directly. So nothing here needs a model; what was missing was the **channel**, and the channel is what was built.

**Limits, stated so the delivery is not over-read.** There is **no model, no provider, no API key, no network call, no cache and no transcript** — `LLM = false` is a constant in the module's own contract and every payload carries `llm:false, model:null, api_key_required:false, network:false, reads_only:true, writes:0, new_data:false, predicts:false, grants_permission:false, gives_direction_or_signal:false, changes_limits:false, authority:'none'`. The channel is **deterministic**: same stored state + same question → same bytes. It **answers from what the app already computed**, it **writes nothing at all**, and it **refuses** the four things the episode forbids plus the two a journal cannot do. A refusal is a successful answer carrying its reason, not an error. The refusal families are **APP policy built from the episode's own words**, not quoted course laws; the 280-character bound, the seven surfaces and the 24-hour clock policy of other surfaces are app policy too.

## Implemented mechanisms

1. **A read-only question router over the app's own findings.** `src/ask.js` (`ask-v1`, 398 lines) routes a bounded question to one of seven owned surfaces: *what the gate currently reads* (M64 routine cycle + stored step acknowledgements, the guardrail day state/caps the entry engine reads, the M106 gate answers, the M107 intention), *my limits and today's risk facts* (account policy, today's selected cap, daily-loss/drawdown/profit-target policy, open positions, today's realised net), *what the app sees in my logged trades* (`Coach.analyse`'s own insights, narrated not recomputed, with the score withheld below the M11 30-trade floor and the reason quoted), *my numbers as history not a forecast* (`P.kpis` with the history-not-forecast note attached), *the records I stored today* (gate + intention + practice + routine), *the rules and checklist I recorded* (the latest recorded rule change, the recorded checklist), and *news/calendar state*. Routing is a bounded classifier plus a deterministic tie-break (a surface's own vocabulary outranks a word it shares with another; ties break on the earlier cue); there is no scoring model, no randomness and no hidden state.
2. **Refusals are answers, with reasons and citations.** Six families, checked in order before any surface: `ask_refused_permission` (should/enter/buy/sell/signal/pick questions), `ask_refused_prediction` (direction, odds, "will price rise", forecasts), `ask_refused_outcome_target` (an amount plus an outcome verb in the same non-negated question — the same pairing M107's classifier refuses, reusing that classifier rather than a second copy; a *negated* mention of an amount stays answerable), `ask_refused_mental_state` ("am I calm/tilted/ready", meditating questions), `ask_refused_other_person` (another person's account or data), and `ask_refused_override` (change a cap, disable the news block, ignore a killzone, unlock risk). Each returns `200` with `refused:true`, the reason, the episode's own words as the citation, and `can_answer` naming what it *can* answer instead. **A refusal never carries surface facts** — there is deliberately no topic attached.
3. **Honest non-answers.** A question that lands on no owned surface returns `answered:false, refused:false, reason:{code:'no_matching_topic', because, topics:[…]}` — "I do not know how to answer that from stored facts, so I am not answering it at all", with the list of surfaces it does own. The news surface answers **unknown** on every day: the calendar is fetched live and never stored, the channel performs no network access, and it will not translate "I could not look" into "no high-impact news". Unknown is not a pass.
4. **Nothing invented; unreadable is reported.** Every line carries a `kind` (`fact|record|unavailable|note`) and the `source` surface it came from. A retained-stop payload that will not parse is `unavailable` with the reason — never "no stops"; malformed `users.settings` yields "your stored settings could not be read, so the checklist is not summarised here (it is not assumed empty)"; a practice row whose measured time cannot be read back is reported and excluded from both the count and the seconds. Records are **quoted with their own timestamps**: the gate line carries the record's `recorded_at`, its age and the gate module's own freshness word instead of asserting that the answers are today's, and the routine line reports the stored cycle's own date rather than refreshing it into today.
5. **Read-only by construction, not by promise.** Every reader is a SELECT or a module's documented read path; the module exports no write helper and no statement is built by concatenation. The practice facts are read **directly** rather than through M108's `Practice.optional`, because that read creates its lazy `practice_timers` row — so asking does not create it. Consequences measured: a full battery (7 surfaces + 6 refusals + 1 non-answer) over a real journal leaves **every table byte-identical**, twice over on a tree with corrupted rows, including `sqlite_sequence`; the `sqlite_master` objects on an inherited database are unchanged; the channel adds **no table, no index, no trigger**; and the backup gains no ask/answer/question/consult key.
6. **No authority and no wiring.** The channel is required in exactly **one** file — the route that exposes it. No engine, bot, guardrail, routine, gate, intention or practice module requires it; entry admission answers exactly as before whether or not the trader was asking; no banner, permission message or guardrail reason mentions it. The entry surface is measured before and after the whole battery with identical statuses and codes.
7. **Two routes, one contract.** `GET /api/ask/topics` serves the surfaces, the refusal codes, the cannot-know list and the meta flags; `POST /api/ask` takes `{question}` **only** — extras in the body (`account_id`, `date`, `from`, `to`, `timezone`) are ignored rather than honoured, and a second trader's workspace is never read: a trader with no account is answered "no account is selected", not the first trader's numbers. A malformed question (empty, whitespace-only, >280 characters, multi-line, control characters, markup) is `400 ask_invalid`; a storage failure inside a reader is answered honestly as `unavailable` with `writes:0`, or `503 ask_unavailable` when the stored facts themselves cannot be read. No answer leaks a filesystem path, a SQLite message, a stack frame, or a stack/SQL fragment for refused input.
8. **The shipped card.** A Dashboard card beside the practice/intention/state cards: the honest framing (*deterministic, read-only, no AI*; where answers come from; what it refuses before you ask), seven example chips, one bounded input (`maxlength=280`) and one read-only send action, answer lines each showing **kind · source**, refusals rendered with code, reason, episode citation and `can_answer`, and a "what this channel will not do" list with the refusal codes. Every answer goes into the **card's own** answer box (two mounted cards never cross-fill), typed text is sent as typed, markup is refused rather than injected (`img` count stays 0, no console error), and a card whose session changed refuses with a toast instead of asking as the new workspace.

## Initial errors kept, not erased by closure

The M108 milestone is already released and the inherited evidence is not restaged. During M109's own build the following were found and fixed, in the order they appeared: the amount-plus-outcome pairing was initially matched without reusing M107's classifier (fixed: the classifier is reused and its cues travel with the refusal); a read request sharing a noun with a decision ("Can I see my risk limits?") was initially refused as a permission question (fixed: decision verbs are unambiguous actions, nouns are not); the entry surface was initially reported merely because the question mentioned a trade, and a refusal could carry a topic (fixed: a refusal has **no** topic); the gate line initially said "for today" about a record that may be two days old (fixed: the record's own timestamp and the gate module's own freshness word are quoted); the intention reader initially mislabelled an **unrecorded** day as an unreadable row and read the wrong field for the saved-example name (fixed: `unrecorded` is the normal case; `template` is the field); two mounted cards initially wrote into the first card's answer box (fixed: a card asks into its own box — the probe that caught it now asserts it). The pre-hardening control tree preserves the three staged faults exactly as measured (14/3 and 28/3), so the fix is a measurement rather than a claim.
