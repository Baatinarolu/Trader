## Implemented contract

1. **Read-only, zero schema.** New `src/behavior-days.js`; no new trade columns/tables/persisted score, backfill, completion/permission write or new proof import. Raw inputs are own accounts/current trades and retained M90 admissions/events; client DTOs/legacy raw trade permission cannot become scoring authority.
2. **UTC ENTRY/rule-day scope.** Current records group on valid UTC `opened_at`; retained actual admissions/exceptions keep their original rule date. Current retrospective close reviews can update an earlier entry day's display. This is NOT UTC close-day realised money or local-time trader/broker hours. Overnight profits don't relocate the original rule-day action. Reopen/correction/account move/deletion leaves retained prior actions visible, but they cannot silently become current fully-covered action evidence.
3. **Evidence required, not absence of flags.** Each current action needs an exactly matching valid actual captured entry cycle plus current complete execution acknowledgement (M86 hash/source/clock) and current complete core acknowledgement (M85 hash/clock/explicit mistake-or-none). Open actions, incomplete/legacy/stale/corrupt/future/pre-close reviews, chronology errors, unknown dates, mismatched or ambiguous capture cycles, orphaned/deleted/prior actions and unavailable/undatable process facts prevent a complete final daily score. Missing history is not clean; imported human words without actual app admission cannot create a score. Known explicit incidents can still be displayed when other evidence is incomplete.
4. **Explicit APP points, outcome-independent.** Per current action/cycle use the **MAX**, not sum, of: actual recorded personal-stop trade exception12; current explicit stop-not-honoured12; current adherence1/2→12,3→6,4→3,5→0; current acknowledged named core mistake6. Source details retained, duplicate admission/event and multiple reports of one action not multiple penalties. Exceptions are a review caution, not automatic verified broker misconduct. Raw day score `max(0,100-known_penalty_points)` only with full coverage and at least one closed action. Known0 is distinct from unknown/null. No net/P&L/R/RR/fees/size/entry model/setup grade/emotion/excursions/stop-movement points; equal current process can show the same tally on green/red/breakeven outcomes. A financial/context correction can honestly stale required acknowledgement; that is evidence freshness, not a P&L-based score penalty.
5. **Tier/trend/legacy separation.** Below30 current closed fully covered actions, daily tier null;30+ may display app vocabulary Disciplined/Solid/Leaking/Undisciplined/Critical, explicitly not edge/statistical trader certification or sizing. Trend is separate account/date points with null gaps, not zero interpolation, account pooling or a replacement for the old window score. Legacy `detectMistakes`/12-6-3 weights/5 detector/30 grade gates remain exact; their old raw score can still be100 with no evidence, so the new UI qualifies it rather than pretending it is a daily process score. Old M103 adaptation work remains partial.
