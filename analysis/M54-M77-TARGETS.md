## M54: catalogue and selection

`src/bots/targets.js` supplies the actual catalogue consumed by `Setup.targetPools` and `buildSetups`:

1. Existing liquidity/structural candidates, including PDH/PDL and weak structural labels.
2. **Opposing order blocks:** opposite direction, explicitly `breached=false` and `mitigated=false`; target the physical near edge. Missing lifecycle flags cannot qualify a new zone.
3. **Opposing unfilled FVGs:** explicitly `filled=false`, finite `fill_pct` in `[0,100)`; target the **remaining unfilled edge**, not an already consumed original edge.

Side/finite-price validation, exact-price deduplication, target kind, origin/lifecycle and physical source provenance are retained. A period such as PDH's **1d timebox** is recorded separately from the **5m/15m detector that observed it**. Unknown source timeframes are not guessed.

The default single target is the **nearest qualified magnet before testing RR**. The verdict headline now agrees with the executable single-target management instead of describing a fictitious partial/runner. Weak structure retains its course label and wins an exact-price tie, but cannot outrank a nearer opposing wall. A wall below 0.9R remains visible rather than disappearing from the catalogue. A selected target below the existing minimum means **no trade**, not a farther weak level, measured move, or RR rescue. No qualified target means no executable target.

The old single-source/addition-only and old selection behavior are retained as explicit research controls (`targetMagnets:false`, stage patches), not silently substituted into the live default. The trader's existing opt-in ladder is not the course's default single-target policy.
